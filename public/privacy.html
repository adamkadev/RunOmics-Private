Privacy Policy

RunOmics · last updated 25 November 2026

RunOmics is a personal running dashboard. It is run by one individual for their own training data and is not offered as a service to the public. The owner can invite a few people to view it, by their Google account.

What data is read

With the owner's explicit consent, RunOmics reads the owner's own health and fitness data from two sources:

  • Garmin Connect — running activities, their GPS tracks, heart rate and derived training metrics.
  • Google Health API — sleep sessions and stages, daily resting heart rate, heart rate variability, and daily step counts, recorded by the owner's own wearable.

Only the account holder's own records are requested. No data about any other person is read.

How it is used

The data is used for one purpose: to display the owner's training and recovery history, and to inform the training plan the application produces. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, employment decisions, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's own training.

Who sees it

The owner, and the people the owner invites. Invited viewers see the same pages: the runs and their maps, the records, the plan, and the recovery charts, sleep and heart rate variability included. Morning check-ins, body measurements, settings and the data export are the owner's alone.

Services that receive data

  • Vercel hosts the application. Its Web Analytics and Speed Insights record each page view (the page's address, which for a run includes its id, the referrer, the browser, device type and country) and how fast the page loaded, without cookies.
  • GitHub stores the data in private repositories, and its Actions run the nightly sync, keeping Garmin's raw activity files in their cache.
  • Firebase (Google) handles sign-in, stores the notes and check-ins written in the application, and delivers its notifications, whose short texts can carry a run's figures or the week's resting heart rate.
  • Google Gemini receives, each night, a run's figures (distance, pace, heart rate, splits, weather), the planned session and the athlete's note on it, to write a short verdict. No GPS track or coordinate is sent.
  • The training plan's AI model receives a summary of the last four weeks: the runs and their notes, check-ins, injuries, goals, age, sex, weight and height, and the Google Health sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate readings. It is Anthropic's Claude, run by hand by the owner, or Google Gemini when the owner asks for it. No GPS track or coordinate is sent.
  • intervals.icu receives the planned workouts, to send them on to the watch.
  • MapTiler serves the maps, so it sees which areas are viewed, and names places from points rounded to about 10 metres or more.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim names a run's town from its first point rounded to about a kilometre, when MapTiler has none.
  • OpenRouteService receives the start point and the places to avoid when a running loop is suggested.
  • Open-Meteo gives the forecast for the runs' usual area, rounded to about 10 kilometres.

Each service handles what it receives under its own terms. None is an advertising or data-broker service, and no data is sold.

Limited use

RunOmics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through the Google Health API is used only for the features above: the recovery charts, the weekly summary notification and the training plan. It is transferred only to the services listed above to provide those features, or as required by law. It is never sold, never used for serving advertisements, and not used by RunOmics to train AI models. Apart from the owner, it is seen only by the people the owner invites.

Where it is stored

Fetched data is stored in private GitHub repositories and shown by a private deployment that only the owner and the people they invite can sign in to. Access credentials are held as encrypted secrets and are never committed alongside the data.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while the owner continues to use the application. The owner can revoke this application's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page, which stops all further reads. On request, stored data is deleted by removing the private repository and its deployment.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google consent screen for this application.

RunOmics is a personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of Google, Fitbit or Garmin.
