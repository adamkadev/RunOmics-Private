Privacy Policy

RunOmics · last updated 11 November 2026

RunOmics is a personal, single-user running dashboard. It is run by one individual for their own training data and is not offered as a service to the public. There are no other users, no customers and no analytics on visitors.

What data is read

With the owner's explicit consent, RunOmics reads the owner's own health and fitness data from two sources:

  • Garmin Connect — running activities, their GPS tracks, heart rate and derived training metrics.
  • Google Health API — sleep sessions and stages, daily resting heart rate, heart rate variability, and daily step counts, recorded by the owner's own wearable.

Only the account holder's own records are requested. No data about any other person is read.

How it is used

The data is used for one purpose: to display the owner's training and recovery history back to them, and to inform the training plan the application produces. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, employment decisions, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's own training.

Limited use

RunOmics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through the Google Health API is never sold, never transferred to third parties except as required by law, and never used for serving advertisements. It is not read by humans other than the account holder.

Where it is stored

Fetched data is stored in a private repository and rendered by a private deployment that only the owner can sign in to. Access credentials are held as encrypted secrets and are never committed alongside the data. Nothing is shared with a third-party analytics, advertising or data-broker service.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while the owner continues to use the application. The owner can revoke this application's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page, which stops all further reads. On request, stored data is deleted by removing the private repository and its deployment.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google consent screen for this application.

RunOmics is a personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of Google, Fitbit or Garmin.
