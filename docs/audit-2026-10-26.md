*26 October 2026 · `RunOmics-Private` at `9043fb1` (v3.2.1 + 550 lines of [Unreleased]) · `RunOmics-data-lamiaa` at `041e01e`*

- **Findings:** 178 raw findings went in (170 from the audits, 8 from the critic). 172 came out confirmed or corrected, and 6 were refuted outright (listed in §7, with a few more the verifiers rejected in part). They are merged below into **about 80 actions**.

## 1. The answer on one page

**The engineering is healthy.** Lint, typecheck and the cycle check pass. 3,557 tests pass with 81 % line coverage (domain 96 %), and 524 Python tests pass in 4 s. The production build takes 33 s, there are 0 known vulnerabilities in production dependencies, and Today's initial JS is about 209 KB gzipped. The sync pipeline is careful: atomic writes, optimistic concurrency and a Python↔TS contract fixture.

**What needs work sits in four places:**

1. **The second athlete's deployment runs on assumptions made for the owner's watch.**
   - Her Forerunner 55 sends no Training Effect, so RunOmics guesses each run's type. The guess files **every walk/run session as "VO2Max"**, and 35 of her 39 runs as quality work. **Six of the twelve auditors found this independently.**
   - From there it spreads: Analytics, heat cost, shoes, the verdict label, the AI verdicts and the coach's adherence (0/5 for a block she completed 5/5) are all wrong.
   - After her 7-month break, the estimated load reads **"Fatigué · above the safe zone"** and can trigger the overreaching push.
   - Her coach has not evaluated a run or reviewed a week since 15 Oct.
   - **Her block ends on 15 Nov** and nothing prompts its renewal. A renewal generated today would also apply rules written for the owner.
2. **The garth sunset is the largest external risk.**
   - Shoes, the Garmin calendar, training status, threshold pace, race predictions and **pushing coach workouts to the watch** all come only through garth.
   - garth tokens can no longer be minted, and they die about a year after minting, on a date that cannot be read.
   - When a token dies, every night turns red with a failure push, and Today keeps showing frozen Garmin readings as if they were current.
3. **Several failure modes give no signal.**
   - `sendPush` records pushes that FCM refused as delivered.
   - Every alert depends on the deployment it is meant to watch.
   - The health endpoint says "stale" when you stop running, not when the sync stops.
   - **The privacy gate fails open:** if one environment variable goes missing, full routes (now stored without privacy zones) become public.
4. **Fast iteration has left debris.**
   - #315 removed the Plan Coach card but left its server work, strings and docs in place.
   - About 20 dead modules or exports remain, and README/docs have drifted.
   - The public-demo export is blocked.
   - Today's scheduled coverage CI run failed, and the E2E apt step hung on 3 recent main runs.
   - 550 changelog lines are unreleased.
